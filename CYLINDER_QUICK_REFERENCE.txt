KEY INSIGHT:
- Cylinder dimensions are AUTO-CALCULATED, not user-entered
- Formulas ensure minimum dimensions are met
- Warnings appear if maximums are exceeded (physical machine limits)
- Circumference comes directly from product height (cutStep)
- Length depends on how many image repeats fit (spreadWidth × numImages)
